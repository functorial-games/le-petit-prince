# Mendelson & Wu (2008) — *Surgical Anatomy of the Lower Face: The Premasseter Space, the Jowl, and the Labiomandibular Fold*

**Reference:** Bryan Mendelson, Woffles Wu, *Aesthetic Plastic Surgery* (2008).

## Summary

Cadaver and surgical observations describe the premasseter space as a defined areolar/gliding plane with membranous boundaries over the lower masseter, connected to the anatomy of the jowl and labiomandibular region.

The work emphasizes that lower-face soft tissue includes true spaces and boundary structures rather than one continuously bonded layer.

## Importance to this repository

This is directly relevant to a heavy pilot.

A convincing jowl/lower-cheek model may require:
- constrained boundaries around a gliding region;
- separate buccal/deep fat contributions;
- platysma/SMAS relationships;
- jawline attachment constraints.

A homogeneous elastic cheek bonded everywhere to the masseter would miss this anatomy.
