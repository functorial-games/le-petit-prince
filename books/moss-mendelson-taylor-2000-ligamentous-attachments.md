# Moss, Mendelson & Taylor (2000) — *Surgical Anatomy of the Ligamentous Attachments in the Temple and Periorbital Regions*

**Reference:** C. J. Moss, B. C. Mendelson, G. I. Taylor, *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery* 105(4), 1475–1490, 2000.

## Summary

This anatomical work maps ligamentous/fascial attachments in the temporal and periorbital regions and is widely cited in later descriptions of facial retaining structures and spaces.

## Importance to this repository

The eye region cannot be modeled as an isolated eyelid patch. Periorbital surface motion occurs within a constrained layered system tied to orbital and temporal supporting structures.

That is especially relevant to:
- the pilot's heavy/tired lower eyelid;
- squinting and eye crinkle;
- the lid-cheek junction;
- orbital fat and retaining-structure interaction.

The geometry should inform constraints; mechanical parameter values still require separate evidence.
