# Rohrich & Pessa (2008) — *The Retaining System of the Face: Histologic Evaluation of the Septal Boundaries of the Subcutaneous Fat Compartments*

**Reference:** Rod J. Rohrich, Joel E. Pessa, *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery* 121(5), 1804–1809, 2008. DOI: 10.1097/PRS.0b013e31816c3c1a. PMID 18454006.

**Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18454006/

## Summary

This histologic study tested the boundaries between subcutaneous facial fat compartments. The authors found fibrous condensations of connective tissue forming barriers between compartments, extending from underlying fascia toward the dermis.

They interpret this interconnecting septal framework as a facial retaining system that limits shear.

## Importance to this repository

This provides a concrete anatomical mechanism by which muscle-generated deformation need not diffuse smoothly across the entire cheek.

For a mechanics model, these septa/retaining structures are candidates for:
- localized constraints on relative shear;
- coupling between deeper fascia and skin;
- boundaries between fat regions.

The paper does **not** give a complete mechanical constitutive characterization of those septa. Geometry and existence are better supported than stiffness parameters.
